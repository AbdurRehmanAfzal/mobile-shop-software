# Mobile Shop — App ka Taaruf

*Dukaan ke maalik, staff, partners aur investors ke liye. Koi technical ilm zaroori nahi.*
Aakhri update: 2026-10-27 · Tayyar karda: OOP Solutions

> **"Har mobile ki poori kahani, har paise ka hisaab, ek aasaan app mein."**

---

## 1. Mobile Shop kya hai?

Mobile Shop ek app hai jo Pakistani mobile dukaan ka saara kaam sambhalti hai: naye aur used phone khareedna bechna, udhaar khata, wapsi, marammat, charger cover, roz ke kharche aur din ka hisaab. Yeh phone, tablet ya laptop par chalti hai, aur **English, Urdu ya Roman Urdu** teeno mein hai.

**27 October 2026** se yeh live hai: **https://mobileshop.oopsolutions.com**. Saath mein **Android app** bhi hai.

### Yeh kaunsa masla hal karti hai?

Zyada tar mobile dukaanein abhi bhi diary, register ya Excel par chalti hain. Is se wohi masle baar baar aate hain:

- **Udhaar gum ho jata hai.** Teen mahine pehle kisi ne aadhe paise de kar phone liya tha. Kis ke naam likha tha? Kitne baqi hain? Diary ka safha phat gaya, ya salesman ne kahin aur likh diya.
- **Used phone mein khatra hai.** Koi gahak apna phone bech kar chala gaya. Ek hafte baad police aa gayi ke phone chori ka tha. Aap ke paas na us ka CNIC hai, na tasweer, kuch saboot nahi ke aap ne imaandari se khareeda.
- **Pata nahi chalta kis ne kya kiya.** A54 Rs. 2,000 kam mein kis salesman ne becha? Yeh phone stock mein kis ne daala?
- **"Galle mein kitna hona chahiye?"** Dukaan band karte waqt cash poora nahi milta aur kisi ko samajh nahi aata kyun.
- **Phone ki kahani kho jati hai.** Wohi phone chhe mahine baad wapas dukaan par aa gaya. Pehle bhi liya tha? Kis se? Koi masla tha?
- **Mushkil software.** Jo software milte hain woh English mein hain, accounting wale lagte hain, aur dukaandar se accounting ke alfaaz samajhne ki umeed rakhte hain.

Mobile Shop yeh sab aasaan screens, bade buttons, dukaan wali zubaan aur khud-ba-khud totals se hal karti hai. Dukaandar ko haath se kuch jama tafreeq nahi karna parta.

## 2. Yeh kis ke liye hai?

| Kaun | App mein kya karta hai |
|---|---|
| **Dukaan ka maalik** | Sab kuch dekhta hai: aaj ki bikri, kharche ke baad asal munafa, kis ne kitna dena hai, stock kitne ka hai, har report. Staff daalta hai, un ka ohda set karta hai, dukaan ki settings badalta hai, ghalat bill khatam karta hai. |
| **Manager** | Counter maalik ki tarah chalata hai: bechta hai, khareedta hai, khareed qeemat aur munafa dekhta hai, bill khatam karta hai, kharche likhta hai. Staff nahi daal sakta, settings nahi badal sakta. |
| **Salesman** | Bechta hai, stock daalta hai, paise leta hai, marammat ke phone leta hai. Khareed qeemat aur munafa **nazar nahi aata**, bill khatam nahi kar sakta, kharche nahi likh sakta. |
| **Karigar (technician)** | Marammat ke kaam karta hai. |
| **Product ka maalik (super admin)** | OOP Solutions. Har customer ke liye nayi dukaan kholta hai aur maalik ko us ka login deta hai. |

**Khud se account nahi banta.** Login page par "dukaan banayein" ka button nahi hai. Jis dukaandar ko app chahiye, woh OOP Solutions se raabta kare (number login page par likha hai), aur product ka maalik us ki dukaan khol deta hai. Is tarah pata rehta hai ke service kaun use kar raha hai.

## 3. Main features

Har feature mein likha hai woh kya karta hai, dukaan ko kyun chahiye, aur ek chhoti si misaal.

### 3.1 Home: dukaan ka counter

**Kya karta hai.** Home screen par maalik ke roz ke sawalon ke jawab seedhe nazar aate hain, koi report kholne ki zaroorat nahi:

- Upar **search**: IMEI, phone ka model ya bande ka naam likhein, ya IMEI barcode scan karein.
- Main kaamon ke **bade buttons**: Bechein, Stock daalein, phir Udhaar, Stock, Marammat, Kharcha aur Wapsi.
- **Hisaab card**, kisi bhi waqt ka: Aaj, Kal, Hafta, Is mahine, ya koi bhi tareekh se tareekh tak. Is mein kul bikri, kitna udhaar par diya, kitna cash aaya, aur **"Haath mein (net)"** (jo paise aaye minus jo gaye) nazar aata hai. Yeh bhi batata hai paise *kahan* gaye (maslan "stock par lage", "wapsi par diye", "kharche").
- **Udhaar khata**: do cards, **"Mujhe lene hain"** (log dukaan ke dene daar) aur **"Mujhe dene hain"** (dukaan dealers waghera ki dene daar).
- **Dukaan mein stock**: stock ke saare phone kitne ke hain, bari raqam lakh mein. Dabayein to brand, phir model ke hisaab se kitne phone aur kitne ke.
- **Munafa** (sirf maalik aur manager ko).
- **Dhyan dein**: jin phones ka shanakhti card check baqi hai, aur marammat ke jo kaam abhi khule hain.

**Kyun zaroori hai.** Maalik counter par ho ya ghar baitha ho, paanch second mein pata chal jata hai dukaan kaisi chal rahi hai.

**Misaal.** Raat 9 baje Imran apne phone par app kholta hai. Aaj: bikri Rs. 186,000, udhaar par diya Rs. 25,000, cash aaya Rs. 161,000. "Mujhe lene hain" mein 12 logon se Rs. 340,000. Dukaan mein stock: Rs. 21.5 lakh.

### 3.2 Stock daalein (phone khareedna)

**Kya karta hai.** Stock teen step mein daalte hain:

1. **Kis se liya?** **Dealer** se (wholesale ya distributor, aam tor par naye ya box pack phone), ya **gahak ka phone** (koi apna phone bechne aaya hai).
2. **Kaun sa phone, kitne ka?** Brand, model, memory, rang (saikron model pehle se list mein hain, apna bhi daal sakte hain), IMEI (likhein ya dabbe ka barcode scan karein), haalat (box pack, open box, used, patch wala), PTA status (PTA approved, Non-PTA, JV; Non-PTA/JV ka SIM time), warranty, khareed qeemat aur bechne ki qeemat. Phir **phone ke saath kya kya aaya**: dabba, charger, taar, handsfree, AirPods. Har cheez ke liye teen bade button: **Nahi · Mil gaya · Baad mein**. "Baad mein" par poochta hai kab dega, taake yaad rahe kya lena baqi hai.
3. **Paise kaise diye?** Cash, Easypaisa/JazzCash/bank account, ya udhaar (agar kuch diye to "abhi kitne diye"). Agar baad mein entry kar rahe hain to *kab* khareeda woh bhi likh sakte hain.

**Gahak ke phone par shanakhti card check.** Jab koi apna used phone bechta hai to app kehti hai check karein phone chori ka na ho:
- CNIC number, CNIC aage aur peeche ki tasweer, bechne wale ki tasweer aur phone ki tasweer;
- ek button jo Punjab Police ka **eGadget** portal kholta hai, wahan IMEI check kar ke nateeja likh dein;
- PTA check (8484 par SMS);
- **iqrar nama** print karein jis par bechne wala sign karta hai ke phone us ka apna hai.

Agar bande ko achhi tarah jaante hain to **"Chhorein, main isay jaanta hoon"** daba sakte hain. Kis ne check chhora, yeh phone ki kahani mein save ho jata hai. Agar phone par **"Chori ka shak, na bechein"** laga ho to sirf maalik ya manager bech sakta hai, aur wajah likhni parti hai.

**Purana stock.** App shuru karte waqt jo phone pehle se dukaan mein hain woh "Purana stock" mein daalte hain (na dealer, na cash). Diary ka purana udhaar har bande par "purana baqaya" ke tor par daal dete hain.

**Misaal.** Bilal ek walk-in gahak Usman se used Galaxy A54 Rs. 52,000 cash mein khareedta hai. Usman ke CNIC, us ki shakal aur phone ki tasweer leta hai, eGadget par IMEI check karta hai (saaf), iqrar nama print kar ke sign karwata hai. Charger saath aaya, dabba nahi ("Nahi"). Bechne ki qeemat Rs. 58,000 rakhta hai. A54 ab stock mein hai.

### 3.3 Bechna (cash ya udhaar)

**Kya karta hai.** Bikri bhi teen step mein: **phone par dabayein → qeemat aur paise kaise mile → ho gaya.**

- Paise: Cash, account (Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank), **Udhaar**, ya **kuch udhaar** (kuch abhi, baqi baad mein). Gahak ka naam sirf udhaar par poocha jata hai.
- Warranty: Koi nahi, 7 din, 1, 3 ya 6 mahine, 1 saal.
- Accessories: **Nahi · De diya · Baad mein**, "Baad mein" ki tareekh ke saath, taake waada kiya hua charger ya dabba yaad rahe.
- Charger, cover ya aur saamaan usi bill mein daal sakte hain.
- Aakhir mein **80mm raseed** ya **A4 bill** print karein.

**Khareed se kam par nahi.** Phone ki bechne ki qeemat kabhi khareed qeemat se kam set nahi ho sakti. Bikri par agar koi khareed se kam qeemat likhe to laal warning aati hai. Salesman ko kaha jata hai qeemat barhaye. Sirf maalik ya manager **"Mujhe pata hai, nuqsan par bechein"** daba sakta hai.

**Misaal.** Do din baad Bilal A54 Ahmed Raza ko Rs. 58,000 mein bechta hai: Rs. 29,000 abhi cash, Rs. 29,000 udhaar. Ahmed ke khate mein Rs. 29,000 lene hain, aur is phone par dukaan ka munafa Rs. 6,000.

### 3.4 Wapsi, dukaan ki katoti ke saath

**Kya karta hai.** Gahak phone wapas laye to IMEI ya naam se (ya haal ki bikri se) phone dhoondein, jo wapas aaya us par tick karein, aur likhein kitne wapas dene hain. **Dukaan ne kitne kaate** woh app khud nikalti hai. Paise cash ya account se wapas dein, ya gahak ke khate mein rakh dein. Phone wapas stock mein aa jata hai, aur din ke hisaab mein "wapsi par diye" aur "wapsi par kaate" nazar aata hai.

**Kyun zaroori hai.** Pakistani dukaanein phone wapas lete waqt aam tor par kuch kaat'ti hain. App isay saaf saaf dukaan ki aamdani likhti hai, galle mein koi pura-asraar farq nahi banta.

**Misaal.** Gahak ne Rs. 100,000 ka phone liya aur agle din wapas le aaya. Dukaan Rs. 90,000 wapas deti hai. App Rs. 10,000 dukaan ki katoti likhti hai, aur phone dobara bechne ke liye stock mein aa jata hai.

### 3.5 Udhaar khata (log aur un ka baqaya)

**Kya karta hai.** Dukaan jin se len den karti hai sab ki ek list: gahak, dealer, doosri dukaanein aur karigar. Har bande ka apna page: baqaya ("Mujhe lene hain" ya "Mujhe dene hain"), **khate ka record** (har bikri, khareedari aur payment), aur bade buttons **Paise mile** / **Paise diye**. Baqaya hamesha record se khud banta hai, koi haath se nahi likhta. Naam, phone ya CNIC se dhoond sakte hain, aur kisi ka khata download kar sakte hain.

**Misaal.** Ahmed Raza Jumme ko Rs. 15,000 deta hai aur agle hafte Rs. 14,000. Har dafa "Paise mile" dabaya. Doosri payment ke baad us ka baqaya **Saaf** dikhata hai.

### 3.6 IMEI ki kahani aur barcode scan

**Kya karta hai.**
- Har phone ki **puri kahani**: kis se khareeda, card check hua, amanat par diya, bika, wapas aaya, marammat ko gaya, qeemat badli; har cheez tareekh, bande, raqam aur kis staff ne kiya ke saath.
- **IMEI check**: IMEI likhein ya scan karein, app batati hai "Naya IMEI, pehle nahi aaya" ya "Yeh IMEI pehle aap ki dukaan mein aa chuka hai, 2 baar khareeda". IMEI ghalat lage to bhi bata deti hai.
- **Barcode scan**: har IMEI box ke saath **Scan** ka button. Phone ka camera dabbe ke label par karein, app IMEI parh leti hai aur dabbe ke baqi barcode chhor deti hai. Label par IMEI 1 aur IMEI 2 dono hon to aap chun lein.

**Kyun zaroori hai.** Kabhi police ya gahak kisi phone ka poochein to dukaan saaf dikha sakti hai phone kahan se aaya aur kahan gaya. Yeh kahani mitayi nahi ja sakti.

**Misaal.** Ek banda Redmi Note 13 bechne aata hai. Bilal scan karta hai: "Yeh IMEI pehle aap ki dukaan mein aa chuka hai". Kahani batati hai ke aath mahine pehle dukaan ne yehi phone isi bande ko 6 mahine ki warranty ke saath becha tha.

### 3.7 Marammat (repair)

**Kya karta hai.** Repair ka kaam chaar marhalon mein: **Phone liya → Theek ho raha hai → Tayyar → Wapas de diya.**
- Phone lete waqt: gahak, phone, kharabi (jaldi wale chips aur apne alfaaz), andazan kharcha, advance, kab tak tayyar. **Parchi** print karein.
- "Theek ho gaya": purze (dukaan ke stock se) aur mazdoori daalein.
- "Gahak ko wapas dein": batata hai kitne lene baqi hain; jo na mile woh gahak ke khate mein chala jata hai.
- Saath mein: "purzon ka intezar", "theek nahi ho saka", aur dukaan ka apna phone bahar ke karigar ko bhejna (karigar ka kharcha us phone ki qeemat mein jur jata hai).

**Misaal.** Sana apna iPhone 11 screen badalne ke liye chhorti hai. Andazan kharcha Rs. 8,500, advance Rs. 2,000, 3 din mein tayyar. Screen lag gayi, kaam tayyar. Lete waqt Rs. 5,000 deti hai, Rs. 1,500 us ke khate mein reh jate hain.

### 3.8 Accessories (charger, cover waghera)

**Kya karta hai.** Charger, taar, cover, glass protector, handsfree, battery, memory card aur marammat ke purzon ka stock: bechne ki qeemat, ausat qeemat, aur "kam se kam" ki had. Accessories akeli bhi bik sakti hain ya phone ke saath usi bill mein. Saath mein **baqi accessories** ki list: dealer ne kya dena hai aur dukaan ne gahak ko kya dena hai, "De diya" ke button ke saath.

**Misaal.** Dukaan ne 20W charger ki "kam se kam" had 5 rakhi. Jab 4 reh gaye to "Stock kam" mein aa gaye, maalik khatam hone se pehle mangwa leta hai.

### 3.9 Kharche aur din band karna

**Kya karta hai.** Kharche qism ke hisaab se likhein (kiraya, bijli, tankhwah, advance, chai waghera; apni qism bhi bana sakte hain). Raat ko **Din band karein** batata hai shuru ka cash, kitna aaya, kitna gaya, aur **galle mein kitna hona chahiye**. Dukaandar cash gin kar likhta hai, app farq dikha deti hai. Kharche maalik ya manager likhta hai.

**Misaal.** Kiraya Rs. 45,000, chai Rs. 600, bijli ka bill Rs. 12,400. Raat ko app kehti hai galle mein Rs. 83,200 hone chahiyein. Manager Rs. 83,000 ginta hai, Rs. 200 ka farq likha jata hai.

### 3.10 Exchange (purana de kar naya)

**Kya karta hai.** Gahak purana phone de kar naya leta hai, ek hi step mein. App hisaab lagati hai: naye phone ki qeemat − purane ki qeemat + pichla baqaya − jo diye = naya baqaya. Purana phone us gahak se khareedari ke tor par stock mein aata hai (apne card check ke saath).

**Misaal.** Kashif apna Vivo Y21 (Rs. 18,000 ka) de kar Rs. 42,000 ka Galaxy A15 leta hai. Rs. 20,000 cash deta hai, Rs. 4,000 us ke khate mein.

### 3.11 Doosri dukaan ko amanat

**Kya karta hai.** Saath wali dukaan aksar phone "gahak ko dikhane" le jati hai. App phone ko us dukaan ke naam **"Amanat par"** likh deti hai (na stock mein, na bika), wapsi ki tareekh ke saath; der ho jaye to "Der ho gayi" dikhati hai. Baad mein ya to **"Phone wapas aa gaya"** dabayein ya **"Unhon ne rakh liya"**. "Rakh liya" us dukaan ko tay shuda qeemat par bikri ban jati hai jo un ke khate mein jati hai.

**Misaal.** Al-Madina Mobiles Tecno Spark 20 amanat par le jati hai. Do din baad Rs. 31,000 mein rakh leti hai aur Rs. 15,000 deti hai. Un ke khate mein Rs. 16,000 lene hain.

### 3.12 Report

**Kya karta hai.** Maalik aur manager ke liye; Aaj, Hafta, Is mahine ya koi bhi tareekh se tareekh tak:
- **Munafa**: bikri, bike hue phones ki khareed, marammat ki aamdani, doosri aamdani (jaise wapsi ki katoti), kharche, aur **kharche ke baad asal munafa**. Jama hua tax bhi.
- **Sab se zyada bika**: kaun se model aur cheezein sab se zyada biki, aur har ek par munafa.
- **Andaza**: agle 7 din ka andaza, pichle 4 hafton ke usi din ki bikri se; aur kaun se phone khatam hone wale hain ("din baqi") ke saath mashwara: aur mangwayein / theek hai / dheema. Saaf likha hai ke yeh andaza hai, pakki baat nahi.
- **Purana stock**: phone kitne din se dukaan mein pare hain.
- **Sales tax**: agar dukaan tax leti hai to tax wali bikri aur jama hua tax (rate, qeemat mein tax shamil ya nahi, bill par NTN/STRN settings mein set hote hain).

**Download.** Har report aur list (din ka hisaab, stock, stock kitne ka, khata, kisi bande ka khata, bikri, khareedari, kharche) par **Download** ka button, teen option: **PDF**, **Excel** aur **CSV** (saada table file jo har spreadsheet khol leti hai).

### 3.13 Staff, ohde aur "kis ne kiya"

**Kya karta hai.** Maalik staff daalta hai aur ohda deta hai (maalik, manager, salesman, karigar), password naya kar sakta hai, aur koi chala jaye to us ka account band kar sakta hai. Har staff apna **jaldi PIN** (4 se 6 hindse) bana sakta hai taake dukaan ke saanjhe phone par jaldi login ho. Har bikri aur stock entry par likha hota hai **kis ne becha** aur **kis ne daala**.

**Misaal.** Maalik dekhta hai ke Galaxy S23 Hamza ne sham 4:15 par becha, aur iPhone 13 kal Zubair ne stock mein daala.

### 3.14 Maalik aur gahak ko paighaam

**Kya karta hai.** Settings mein dukaan chunti hai kis ko kab paighaam jaye: har bikri ya stock daalne par maalik ko, bikri ya payment par gahak ko, khareedari par bechne wale ko. "Phone tayyar hai" ka paighaam bhi banta hai. Abhi app paighaam tayyar karti hai aur dukaandar ek tap se WhatsApp par bhej deta hai. Khud-ba-khud bhejna planned hai (section 6 dekhein).

### 3.15 Teen zubaanein, Light aur Dark

- **English, Urdu aur Roman Urdu.** Ek waqt mein sirf ek zubaan nazar aati hai. Urdu sahi Nastaliq mein, daayein se baayein. Number hamesha aam hindson mein (1, 2, 3).
- Print hone wali raseed aur iqrar nama dukaan ki chuni hui zubaan mein (English ya Urdu).
- Home se ek tap mein **Light aur Dark**.
- Har button par icon *aur* naam dono, taake andaza na lagana pare.

### 3.16 Super admin (OOP Solutions ke liye)

Yeh page sirf product ke maalik ko nazar aata hai. Is mein saari customer dukaanon ki list aur **"Nayi dukaan kholein"**: dukaan ka naam, maalik ka naam, maalik ka login mobile aur password, dukaan ki zubaan. Aakhir mein login tafseel nazar aati hai jo maalik ko deni hai, **WhatsApp par bhejein** ke button ke saath.

### 3.17 Phone app, website aur laptop

- **Website**: kisi bhi browser mein https://mobileshop.oopsolutions.com kholein, phone, tablet ya laptop par. Android Chrome par "Add to Home screen" bhi kar sakte hain, phir app ki tarah khulti hai.
- **Android app (APK)**: APK Android app ki file hoti hai jo Play Store ke baghair seedha install hoti hai. Yeh ek asli phone par install kar ke live system ke saath test ho chuki hai. *Abhi ki kami*: Android app ke andar print aur download abhi kaam nahi karte; un ke liye website use karein.
- **Laptop**: bari screen par kaam side menu mein aate hain, aur counter par jaldi kaam ke liye keyboard shortcuts (F-keys).

- **11:30, dealer ka maal.** Dealer se paanch box pack Infinix Hot 40, Rs. 33,500 har ek. Hamza har dabbe ka IMEI scan karta hai, "Dealer se" chunta hai, Rs. 100,000 cash diye aur baqi udhaar. Dealer ke khate mein **Rs. 67,500 dene hain**.
- **12:15, gahak apna phone bechne aaya.** Usman used Galaxy A54 Rs. 52,000 mein bechta hai. Hamza CNIC aur tasweerein leta hai, eGadget check (saaf), iqrar nama print kar ke sign karwata hai.
- **14:00, aadha udhaar.** Purana gahak Ahmed Raza Infinix Hot 40 Rs. 38,000 mein leta hai: Rs. 19,000 cash, Rs. 19,000 udhaar. Hamza raseed print karta hai.
- **15:30, marammat.** Sana screen badalne ke liye phone chhorti hai: andazan kharcha Rs. 8,500, advance Rs. 2,000. Parchi print hoti hai.
- **16:00, wapsi.** Gahak kal ka Rs. 40,000 wala phone wapas lata hai. Dukaan Rs. 37,000 wapas deti hai. Rs. 3,000 ki katoti likhi jati hai aur phone wapas stock mein.
- **17:00, Bilal pohanchta hai** aur apne phone par app kholta hai. Aaj ki bikri dekhta hai, kis ne kya becha, aur yeh ke Hamza ne ek phone khareed se kam par bechne ki koshish ki. App ne rok diya, to Bilal ne purana stock nikalne ke liye khud Rs. 500 nuqsan par manzoori de di.

- **21:30, din band.** App batati hai galle mein kitna hona chahiye. Bilal ginta hai, raqam likhta hai, din band. Ghar ja kar **Report → Munafa** mein mahine ka hisaab aur **Andaza** dekhta hai: Infinix Hot 40 taqreeban 4 din mein khatam ho jayega, to dealer ko call kar deta hai.

## 5. Hifazat aur bharosa

- **Har dukaan ka data alag.** Har dukaan ko sirf apne phone, log aur paise nazar aate hain. Ek dukaan kabhi doosri ka data nahi dekh sakti.
- **Har raat backup.** System har raat poora backup leta hai aur pichle 14 din ke rakhta hai. (Server se bahar ek aur copy rakhna planned hai.)
- **Khata kabhi edit nahi hota.** Paison ka record na badalta hai na mitta hai. Ghalti ho jaye to ek **nayi durust karne wali entry** lagti hai aur asal record wahin rehta hai. Baqaya hamesha isi record se banta hai, is liye har raqam ki wajah dikhayi ja sakti hai.
- **Phone ki kahani mitayi nahi ja sakti.** Har waqia (khareeda, check hua, bika, wapas aaya, amanat, marammat) hamesha ke liye save hai. Jis bande ya cheez ka record ho, use "mitane" par woh sirf chhup jati hai; record mehfooz rehta hai.
- **Sirf maalik wale kaam.** Bill khatam karna, kharche likhna, khareed qeemat aur munafa dekhna, khareed se kam bechna, aur "chori ka shak" wala phone bechna sirf maalik aur manager kar sakte hain. Staff daalna aur dukaan ki settings sirf maalik ke paas hain. Jahan zaroori hai wajah likhni parti hai, aur karne wale ka naam save hota hai.
- **Used phone ka saboot.** CNIC ki tasweerein, bechne wale ki tasweer, eGadget ka nateeja aur sign kiya hua iqrar nama hamesha phone ke saath rehte hain.
- **Mehfooz connection.** Website https (mehfooz connection, jaise banks use karte hain) par chalti hai, to phone aur server ke darmiyan data mehfooz rehta hai.
